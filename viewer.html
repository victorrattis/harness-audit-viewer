OpenCode · Audit

Audit log viewer

Drop the folder with the logs here (the audit root, or a single session), or pick it below.

Everything runs locally in the browser — no data leaves the machine. Once the folder is open, ⟳ re-reads it — new turns included — without asking again, and ↻ brings it back on the next visit.
Alternative: python3 -m http.server 8000 in the folder, then open localhost:8000/viewer.html (it loads by itself).
Formats: OpenCode (SSE stream) and harness-audit (final response).
Open opencode DB reads what opencode itself stores, no plugin needed: ~/.local/share/opencode/opencode.db — pick opencode.db-wal with it, or the newest turns stay out (⌘⇧. shows the hidden folder, ⌘⇧G takes the path). It has the messages, tool calls, tokens and cost, but no system prompt, tool schemas or headers. Loads sql.js from cdnjs.
